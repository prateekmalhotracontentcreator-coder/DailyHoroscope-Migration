# SEO 30-Day Launch Plan

Date: 2026-05-22
Scope: SEO-20K umbrella commission

## Week 1 - Technical fixes

- Ship shared SEO component improvements:
  - canonical
  - hreflang
  - JSON-LD support
- Publish dynamic sitemap endpoints for live programmatic route groups
- Add sitemap index and update robots.txt
- Add route-level cache headers in `vercel.json`
- Build and verify City Panchang and Choghadiya routes

## Week 2 - Content seeding and route rollout

- Expand Panchang city coverage through sitemap discovery
- Monitor indexed city/date combinations in GSC
- Prepare M2 compatibility and remedy page content logic
- Validate that Panchang and Choghadiya city slugs match the existing backend location catalogue exactly

## Week 3 - Distribution and link triggers

- Add share-card and internal-link opportunities from Panchang and calendar surfaces
- Link new city Panchang pages from festival, calendar, and hora surfaces where relevant
- Review high-intent internal link paths:
  - Panchang to Choghadiya
  - Choghadiya to Panchang
  - Panchang to festivals

## Week 4 - Monitoring and iteration

- Record keyword baseline in GSC and Bing Webmaster Tools
- Review crawl coverage for:
  - Panchang city URLs
  - Choghadiya city URLs
  - Sign horoscope URLs
- Identify cities or route patterns with weak crawl or thin engagement
- Prioritize M2 route groups based on early crawl behavior
